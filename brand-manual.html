02 · The mark

A frame that holds a meeting.

The monogram is one continuous shape: an M whose legs fold inward and nearly touch. That is where the brand splits in two. The frame is the Studio. The gap in the middle is the person.

THE FRAME · STUDIO

Two 96-unit stems and a roof. Structure, rigour, the release pipeline.

The crossing · Matt

Two arrows, code and design, pointing at each other and not quite touching. The spark is the gap between them.

the grid · shared

One geometry for both identities. Never redraw, stretch or round the mark.

06 · Typography

Wide for the room, cursive for the hand.

The Studio speaks in Archivo Expanded: broad, heavy and uppercase, like a label on a box. Matt writes in Instrument Serif Italic and signs in JetBrains Mono. Inter carries body text for both, so long reading stays neutral.

Studio · Archivo · wdth 125 · 800–900 Aa Qq ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ 0123456789

Headlines, product names, UI labels on Studio surfaces. Tracking −3% at display sizes, +10% on small caps.

Serif for headlines and voice, mono for the handle, code, dates and metadata. No uppercase serif, ever.

07 · Usage

Keep the two rooms apart.

Most mistakes come from mixing the two identities. If a piece looks like it could belong to either one, it's wrong.

DO

Studio: solid white mark inside the safelight tile.

DO

Person: white line mark on ink, followed by one Signal dot.

DON'T

Put the line mark in the tile. That mixes the person with the studio.

DON'T

Add the Signal dot to the Studio tile. The dot is Matt's alone.

DON'T

Stretch, squash or change the tile's corner radius.

DON'T

Recolour the tile. Game key art can change; the tile stays safelight.

DON'T

Use more than one Signal per view, or use it as a fill or background.

Mattq Studios
DON'T

Set the Studio name in the serif, or Matt's voice in Archivo Expanded.
